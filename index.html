⚡ About SurRon Bikes

SurRon bikes are high-performance electric off-road motorcycles designed to deliver power, speed, and reliability while producing zero emissions. Known for their lightweight construction, instant torque, and low-maintenance electric drivetrain, SurRon bikes have become a popular choice among trail riders, adventure enthusiasts, and electric mobility fans around the world.

Whether you're looking for a bike for recreational riding, off-road exploration, or thrilling performance, SurRon offers a range of models to suit different riding styles and experience levels. With advanced battery technology, durable engineering, and responsive handling, SurRon continues to redefine the future of electric off-road riding.

Experience the next generation of riding with SurRon—where innovation meets performance.

SURRON STORM BEE ELECTRIC BIKE

The SurRon Storm Bee comes standard with a multi-level, adjustable energy BERS (E-Brake Energy Return System) system to regenerate the battery during braking. Enjoy extra range and enjoy the ride without the anxiety.

ASR (Active Spin Reduction): The ASR traction control system was independently developed by Surron. With multiple levels of control and on-the-fly adjustability, the Storm Bee can adapt to a variety of road conditions for optimal grip and improved safety.

POWERFUL & INTELLIGENT

SurRon Storm Bee is a power system with continuous output power of 5kW, including 5kW PMSM motor and 5kW FOC motor controller. The product can provide two rated voltage operating modes, 48V and 60V(Rated voltage expansion :48V/60V/72V/84V) which can be automatically adjusted according to the battery system. Due to the excellent heat dissipation structure design and multi-level temperature control strategy, the power system is able to make continuous stable large-scale power output under natural air cooling conditions, which has been applied to the SURRON light bee model with mass production.
