SRTC + BERS assist systems

Instructions: Settings and ASR Setup

1. Power On and Enter Settings Mode

  • With the kickstand down, power on the bike.
  • Hold the Ready and ASR buttons (front-facing buttons on the right and left grips) for 3 seconds until the display shows a letter B and a number.

2. Navigating Settings

  • Tap ASR to move to the next setting.
  • Tap Ready to change the setting value.

3. Settings Overview

  • BO B5: Brake regeneration (activates when battery is under 95% in Sport mode)
  • EO E5: Engine coasting regeneration
  • A1 A3: Anti-Slip traction control (ASR)
  • PO P1: Pause throttle when brake is applied
  • 8 C1: Crash cutoff (C1 disables motor if bike tilts too far)
  • F1 F3: Throttle sensitivity in Sport mode
  • TO T1: Testing mode (records diagnostics)
  • U1 U3: Charging speed control for utility power

How to Set Up ASR (Anti-Slip Traction Control)

1. Activating ASR

To enable ASR for steep slopes or slippery conditions, press the ASR button three times in a row. The ASR light will turn green to indicate it’s active.

2. ASR Strength Levels

The right-side instrument shows ASR strength: One bar for lightest assistance (still allows short wheelies), or three bars for maximum traction control.

3. Adjusting ASR While Riding

  • To toggle ASR on/off while riding: press the ASR button three times.
  • To adjust strength while riding: hold ASR and then double-click the reverse (r & s) button.
